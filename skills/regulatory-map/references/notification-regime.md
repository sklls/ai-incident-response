# Notification regime — reference (regulatory-map)

## India — the clocks, tightest first
| Trigger | Instrument | Who to tell | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Any cyber incident (breach, leak, unauthorised access, system compromise) | CERT-In Directions 2022 (IT Act §70B) | CERT-In | **6 hours** of noticing |
| Personal-data breach | DPDP Act 2023 | Data Protection Board — initial intimation | **without delay** |
| Personal-data breach | DPDP Act 2023 | Data Protection Board — detailed report | **72 hours** |
| Personal-data breach | DPDP Act 2023 | each affected Data Principal | **without delay** |
| Sensitive personal data, negligence | IT Act §43A / SPDI Rules | civil liability | — |
| Agent action / attribution | IT Act §11 | anchors who is liable | — |
| BFSI AI | RBI FREE-AI (2025) | institution accountability | — |

Clock starts at **awareness**, not after investigation. Use an **initial** report to meet a deadline; follow with the detailed one.

## DPDP breach report — required contents
nature of the breach · data categories affected · approximate number of Data Principals · likely consequences · measures taken/proposed.

## EU (comparison) — EU AI Act Art. 73 serious-incident tiers
| Case | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Default serious incident | ≤ **15 days** |
| A death may have been caused | ≤ **10 days** |
| Widespread / serious-irreversible critical-infrastructure disruption | ≤ **2 days** |
Initial incomplete report permitted (Art. 73(5)); Art. 72 = post-market monitoring; Annex III = high-risk (incl. hiring). GDPR Art. 33 = 72h breach notice.

## US (comparison)
EEOC / Title VII (employment); four-fifths + significance for AI selection tools (EEOC 2023).

## OECD common AI-incident harm types (cross-jurisdiction)
harm to persons · disruption of critical infrastructure · violation of rights or law · harm to property/environment.
